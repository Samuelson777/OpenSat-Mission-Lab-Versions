# OpenSat Mission Lab v1.4.0

## Conjunction safety and autonomous replanning

v1.4 adds a deterministic home-PC demonstration of space-situational-awareness workflows and safety-aware constellation operations.

### Added

- Six-object seeded relative-motion screening catalogue
- Protected-spacecraft conjunction screening and time-of-closest-approach reports
- Miss distance, relative speed, uncertainty proxy, risk score, and risk level
- Along-track avoidance-maneuver trade studies
- Post-maneuver miss-distance, propellant, lead-time, and imaging-conflict estimates
- Safety blackout windows around recommended maneuvers
- Utility-based coordinated task optimization
- Autonomous replanning after a spacecraft outage and target-priority escalation
- Interactive constellation safety dashboard
- Four new verification requirements and six focused safety tests

### Scope

The resident-object catalogue, probability values, covariance values, and maneuver displacement model are deterministic educational surrogates. They are not operational conjunction data messages, collision probabilities, orbit-determination products, or maneuver commands.
