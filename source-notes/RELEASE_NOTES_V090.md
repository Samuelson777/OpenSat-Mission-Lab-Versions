# OpenSat Mission Lab v0.9.0 — Reliability Engineering

v0.9 adds a preliminary reliability-engineering layer to the complete v0.8 mission-control digital twin.

## Highlights

- 12-row FMEA with initial and residual risk-priority numbers
- Configurable, seeded Monte Carlo campaign and mission-success criteria
- Mission risk register and mission-objective fault tree
- Explainable engineering-limit and robust-statistical anomaly detection
- Consolidation of recurring events into operator-level anomaly records
- Risk matrix, Monte Carlo outcomes, anomaly timeline, and fault-tree plots
- Self-contained reliability dashboard and machine-readable CSV/JSON products
- 52 automated tests

The bundled 1,000-trial scenario estimates 62.20% mission success under the deliberately marginal demonstration assumptions. This is a surrogate preliminary uncertainty model, not flight probabilistic risk assessment.
